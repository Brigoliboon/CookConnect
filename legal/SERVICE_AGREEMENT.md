**III.2** The Customer shall provide complete and accurate information during registration.

## IV. PAYMENT

**IV.1** The Customer shall pay the Meal Plan fees either:

(a) in full before the commencement of the Subscription; or

(b) through an approved **installment payment plan** offered by Cook Connect.

**IV.2** Where an installment payment plan has been approved, the Customer agrees to pay each installment on or before its scheduled due date.

**IV.3** Cook Connect may provide a grace period for overdue installment payments in accordance with its payment policy or as otherwise communicated to the Customer.

**IV.4** If any installment remains unpaid after the applicable grace period, Cook Connect reserves the right to suspend the Customer's Subscription, including the preparation and delivery of meals, until all outstanding amounts have been paid in full.

**IV.5** During any suspension resulting from overdue payment, missed deliveries shall not be replaced, credited, or refunded unless otherwise approved by Cook Connect in writing.

**IV.6** Once all outstanding payments have been received, the Subscription may resume from the next available delivery date, subject to Cook Connect's production schedule and operational availability.

**IV.7** Unless otherwise expressly stated, all prices and fees charged by Cook Connect are **inclusive of applicable Value Added Tax (VAT)**.

## V. MEAL PLANS

**V.1** Meals are prepared from Cook Connect's rotating menu.

**V.2** Customers may either:

(a) receive the Chef's recommended weekly meal rotation; or

(b) select meals from the weekly menu before the published selection deadline.

**V.3** If no meal selections are received before the applicable deadline, Cook Connect shall prepare meals based on the Chef's recommended rotation.

**V.4** Cook Connect reserves the right to substitute meals with alternatives of comparable quality and nutritional value where ingredients become unavailable or operational circumstances require.

**VI.1** Cook Connect will make reasonable efforts to accommodate dietary preferences and ingredient exclusions.

**VI.2** Requests remain subject to ingredient availability, production schedules, food safety requirements, and operational capacity.

**VI.3** Requests requiring significant recipe modifications or premium ingredients may incur additional charges or require enrolment in a customized meal plan.

**VII.4** Cook Connect shall not be responsible for delays arising from traffic conditions, severe weather, government restrictions, building access limitations, or other events beyond its reasonable control.
